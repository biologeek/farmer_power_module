Farmer Power - Purchase bridge

From the crop plan to your seed orders - Free

Turn the seed needs of a season into draft requests for quotation, one per supplier, in one click.

Main features

One request per supplier

  • One line per variety, from the seed needs report of the crop plan
  • Available from the season form or Planning > Create Seed Requests for Quotation

Seeds and plants

  • Seeds in grams (converted to the unit of the product) or counted in seeds
  • Bought plants through the plant product of the crop template

Safe to run again

  • Quantities already requested for the season are deducted
  • If the plan grows, only the difference is requested
  • Cancelling a request makes its quantity requestable again

Clear warnings

  • Varieties without supplier or without product are skipped and listed
  • The crop manager does not need access to Purchase

How it works

  • Set the supplier and the product on each variety (Farmer Power catalog), then open the season and use the button.
  • The requests are drafts: review them in Purchase before sending them.
  • Seed margin and germination rate come from the seed needs of Farmer Power.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19, Community or Enterprise.
  • Farmer Power (farmer_power) and the Odoo Purchase app.
  • Installed automatically as soon as both are present; uninstalling it leaves Farmer Power untouched.
  • Translations: French, Spanish, German, Dutch, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese.

About

This module is free (LGPL-3). It is a companion of Farmer Power, which is required.
