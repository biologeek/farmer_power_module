Farmer Power - Point of Sale bridge

Market sales in your crop profitability - Free

Market sales rung up in the Point of Sale count in the revenue and the profitability of your crops, exactly like customer invoices.

Main features

Attribution like invoices

  • Pro rata of the monthly harvests of the product (default)
  • Or exactly by lot, when the lot names typed on the POS line are crop lots

Reliable amounts

  • Only paid or posted POS orders; drafts and cancelled orders are ignored
  • Tax-excluded amounts, converted to the company currency
  • Refunds count negatively

No double counting

  • An order already invoiced is counted once, by its invoice (pro rata method)
  • Safe to mix invoices and POS sales

Where you see it

  • "Sales" button of the crop
  • Profitability report and dashboard (monthly revenue)
  • Crop managers see the amounts, not the POS orders

How it works

  • Add the bridge: nothing to configure. The attribution method is the one chosen in the Farmer Power settings.
  • For the by-lot method, track the product by lots in the Point of Sale and type the crop lot name on the line.
  • Tested at scale: 40,000 POS orders and 8,300 crops.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19, Community or Enterprise.
  • Farmer Power (farmer_power) and the Odoo Point of Sale app.
  • Installed automatically as soon as both are present; uninstalling it leaves Farmer Power untouched.
  • No text of its own to translate: it reuses the screens of Farmer Power.

About

This module is free (LGPL-3). It is a companion of Farmer Power, which is required.
