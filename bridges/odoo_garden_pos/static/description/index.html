Farmer Power - Point of Sale bridge

Companion of Farmer Power, installed automatically when Farmer Power and Point of Sale are both installed. Market sales rung up in the Point of Sale count in the revenue and the profitability of the crops.

  • Paid or posted POS order lines, attributed like customer invoices: pro rata of the monthly harvest of the product, or exactly by lot when the lot names typed on the line are crop lots.
  • Refunds count negatively.
  • An order already invoiced is never counted twice.
  • Shown in the Sales button of the crop, the profitability report and the dashboard.
