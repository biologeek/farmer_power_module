Odoo Garden - Purchase bridge

Companion of Odoo Garden, installed automatically when Odoo Garden and Purchase are both installed. It turns the seed needs of a season into draft requests for quotation.

  • One request per supplier, one line per variety, from the seed needs report of the crop plan.
  • Seeds in grams (converted to the unit of the product) or counted in seeds; bought plants through the plant product of the crop template.
  • Varieties without supplier or without product are skipped and listed as warnings.
  • Safe to run again: quantities already requested for the season are deducted.
  • The crop manager does not need access to Purchase.

Menu: Market Gardening > Planning > Create Seed Requests for Quotation, or the button on the season.
